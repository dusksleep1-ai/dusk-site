01 / THE IDEA

A better night starts
under the covers.

Temperature can make a bed feel wrong before sleep even begins. DUSK puts comfort back in your hands.

02 / THE SYSTEM

Simple on the bed.
Smart beside it.

01

A thin topper

A water loop in a mattress topper moves warmth away or brings it back, without blasting air across your bed.

02

Cooling and heating

A bedside unit is designed to control the water temperature, letting you choose cooler or warmer sleep.

03

A gentler wake-up

A planned timer could change the temperature as morning approaches. Wake-up temperature control is part of the v1 concept.

03 / INSIDE THE IDEA

The comfort you feel.
The engineering you don't.

The water path and the power path stay separate. Soft tubing carries heat through the topper. The electrical parts sit inside the bedside unit, not under your sheets.

01 / THE PAD

Cooling where it counts.

A thin topper would hold a soft silicone water loop close to the sleep surface. The aim: spread heating or cooling across the bed without a blast of air.

02 / THE CHILLER

Water does the work.

A bedside tank and pump would circulate water through the loop. A thermoelectric module would change its temperature; a heat sink and fan would manage heat inside the unit.

03 / THE CONTROL

A setting, not a guess.

A temperature sensor and controller would adjust the system toward your chosen setting. Internal wiring and a power supply stay in the enclosed bedside unit.
