01 / THE IDEA

A better night starts
under the covers.

Temperature can make a bed feel wrong before sleep even begins. DUSK puts comfort back in your hands.

02 / HOW IT COULD WORK

Simple on the bed.
Smart beside it.

01

A thin topper

A water loop in a mattress topper moves warmth away or brings it back, without blasting air across your bed.

02

Cooling and heating

A bedside unit is designed to control the water temperature, letting you choose cooler or warmer sleep.

03

A gentler wake-up

A planned timer could change the temperature as morning approaches. Wake-up temperature control is part of the v1 concept.

03 / WHY DUSK

Not a fan.
Real temperature control.

Choose the temperature you want for your bed. DUSK is designed to move heated or cooled water through a thin topper and hold your setting, rather than just blowing air across you. Built around affordable comfort and low running costs. Still a concept, with the waitlist open now.
